Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Screelt ("the App") is a screen recorder for Android. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data collected

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no account system and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Screen and audio recordings

The App records the screen and, if the microphone permission is granted, captures audio while recording. All recordings are saved locally on your device under Movies/Screelt. They are never uploaded to any server and are fully under your control.

Permissions

  • Record screen (MediaProjection): used only to capture the screen content you choose to record.
  • Microphone: used only to capture audio during a recording. If you deny this permission, the App still records video without audio.
  • Notifications: used to show the recording status while a recording is active.

Internet

The App does not require or use an internet connection and contains no networking code.

Third parties

The App shares no data with any third party because it collects no data.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be published here with a new "last updated" date.
